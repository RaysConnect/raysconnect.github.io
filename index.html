FROM DOWNLOAD TO CONNECTED

Start in three steps.

  1. 01

    Install the app

    Choose the package for your device and open Rays Connect.

  2. 02

    Add your profile

    Import the connection profile supplied by your server administrator.

  3. 03

    Choose a mode. Connect.

    Start SOCKS or system VPN. Approve your operating system’s VPN setup when prompted.

Bring your own server.

Rays Connect is a connection client and server toolkit. Installing the app does not include a hosted VPN subscription. Availability and performance depend on your server, network and transport.
